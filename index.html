    Filter effectiveness

    Every filter has to earn its place

    Each rejected signal is still followed as a shadow trade, as if we had taken it. That shows, in R, what every filter saves or costs. A filter that blocks more winners than losers gets loosened or removed.

    When the market turns choppy

    • Adaptive thresholdIf recent signals across the whole market keep failing, the score needed to trade rises on its own.
    • Loss streak pauseAfter a set number of losses in a row, the module stops for the day.
    • Max daily lossA hard cap in % of equity. Hit it and nothing new opens until tomorrow.
    • Cooldown per coinA coin that just failed a breakout is left alone for a while.
