    Scene 06 / your question

    10 trades. 1 winner. What happens?

    Profit does not come from winning often. It comes from losing small and keeping the big moves. Move the controls and see the result after fees.

    Size of the winning move
    Distance from entry to the first stop. 1R = this distance.

    Scene 07 / filter effectiveness

    Every filter has to earn its place.

    Each rejected signal is still followed as a shadow trade, as if we had taken it. That shows, in R, what every filter saves or costs. A filter that blocks more winners than losers gets loosened or removed.

    Scene 08 / proof before live money

    Tested on real data. Checked by you.

    You will not be asked to trust a description. Every step produces numbers you can check, and every trade is listed so you can find it on the chart yourself.

    STEP 01

    Real Bybit data

    6-12 months of trades, M5 candles, open interest and funding for all USDT perps. Order book and liquidations recorded live from day one.

    STEP 02

    Mark every big move

    Every real +5%, +20% and +80% day is marked. Then we measure: caught or missed, how early, and how much of the move was left.

    STEP 03

    Walk-forward test

    Tune on the first months, test on months the logic has never seen. If results break on unseen data, it is overfitted, and we fix it.

    STEP 04

    Real costs

    Taker fees on both sides, slippage from order book depth, and funding. No result is shown without them.

    STEP 05

    Your bot as backtester

    The price, volume and OI logic also runs through your existing bot's backtest, in the tool you already use.

    STEP 06
